How to Play

The rules

You'll need the Friend or Fraud deck — 288 question cards, dry-erase cards and markers. This app replaces the spinner and the scorepad.

  1. Split into pairs. Two people who think they know each other. With an even headcount everyone pairs up; with exactly two players you go head to head instead.
  2. Spin the wheel. It lands on one of five categories. Draw a question card from that category.
  3. Both answer. One player answers about themselves for real. Their partner writes what they think the answer is — no peeking, no hints.
  4. Compare. Same answer? That's a point. Different? No point, and the card goes to the bottom of the deck.
  5. Pass it on. Next pair takes a turn. Play as long as you like — highest score at the end wins.

The categories

Playing online

Everyone joins the host's room code on their own phone. The answerer types the real answer, then the guesser gets 15 seconds to type theirs. The app checks them automatically and is forgiving about spelling — cairokee and Cairokee both count. If it's a near miss like barca versus barcelona, the host makes the call.

House rules

Vague answers are frauds. "Between Me & You" gets spicy — skip it if the room isn't ready. And if two people argue about whether an answer counts, the host decides and everyone moves on.
